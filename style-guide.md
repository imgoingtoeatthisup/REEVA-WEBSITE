# Reeva — Style Guide

## Colors
- Primary: #1B3A57 (dark navy — card backgrounds, headings)
- Secondary: #F5A623 (gold/amber — buttons, accents, matches logo roof shape)
- Accent: #FFFFFF (white — text on dark backgrounds, negative space)

## Vibe
Corporate, trustworthy, technical.

## Typography
Poppins (Google Fonts) — clean geometric sans-serif, slightly more rounded/friendly than Inter, fits a technical services company without feeling cold.

## Notes
- Logo navy reads slightly darker/more saturated (~#1C3A5E) than card-background navy (~#1B3F5E) in the old site — treat as one primary color, don't pixel-match.
- Gold in logo and gold buttons both fall in the #F5A623–#F7A828 range — use #F5A623 as the standard.
