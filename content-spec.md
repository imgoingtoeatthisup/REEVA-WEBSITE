Image Naming & Captioning Guide

How to name the files

Use this pattern for every photo, all lowercase, hyphens not spaces:
page-section-description-number.jpg

-   hero-main-01.jpg

-   services-cctv-01.jpg

-   services-waterproofing-before-01.jpg /
    services-waterproofing-after-01.jpg

-   projects-ubora-exterior-01.jpg

-   projects-medalist-before-01.jpg / projects-medalist-after-01.jpg

-   about-team-01.jpg

Increase the number (02, 03...) if you take multiple shots of the same
thing and want to pick the best later. Keep raw/unused shots in a
separate \'unused\' subfolder so the main folder only holds what\'s
actually going on the site.

How to write the captions

One rule matters more than any other: only name a specific building in a
caption when you are certain that exact photo is from that exact
building. A wrong caption is worse than no caption --- if a client ever
asks about \'their\' photo and it\'s actually a different site, that\'s
a credibility problem, not a small mistake.

-   Real, confirmed photo → \"Installed at \[Building Name\]\" (e.g.
    \"Installed at The Medalist, Sports City\")

-   Real photo, but unsure which building → leave it uncaptioned, or use
    a generic caption like \"Recent installation\"

-   Stock photo → always caption it \"Example installation\" --- never
    let a stock photo imply it\'s a real Reeva job

-   Before/after pairs → caption each side plainly: \"Before\" /
    \"After\", with the building name only if confirmed

Keep captions short --- 3--6 words is plenty. The photo is doing the
work; the caption is just confirming what it\'s proof of.

Jargon Glossary

*Several service names use industry acronyms visitors may not know.
Build note: add these as hover tooltips on first use within the Services
page (small ⓘ icon or dotted underline), rather than making people
scroll to a glossary --- but keep this list as the reference text for
whoever builds the tooltips.*

-   CCTV --- Closed-Circuit Television. The security camera system
    itself.

-   NVR --- Network Video Recorder. The device that stores and manages
    footage from CCTV cameras.

-   ANPR --- Automatic Number Plate Recognition. Cameras that read car
    license plates, usually for gate/parking access.

-   ACS --- Access Control System. Card, fob, or biometric systems that
    control who can open a door.

-   GB --- Gate Barrier. The automatic arm/boom that opens for cars at a
    parking entrance.

-   IC / ICS --- Intercom / Intercom System. Video or audio entry
    systems at a building\'s door.

-   SLD / SLDS --- Sliding Door(s) / Sliding Door System.

-   SHD / SHDS --- Shutter Door(s) / Shutter Door System.

-   PSU --- Power Supply Unit. The component that powers a camera or
    device.

-   DVR --- Digital Video Recorder, an older-generation version of an
    NVR.

-   UPS --- Uninterruptible Power Supply. A battery backup that keeps
    equipment running during a power cut.

-   PPM --- Planned Preventive Maintenance. Scheduled maintenance visits
    done before something breaks, not after.

-   AMC --- Annual Maintenance Contract. A yearly agreement for ongoing
    maintenance visits.

-   ELV --- Extra-Low Voltage. The category covering CCTV, access
    control, intercoms and similar low-power systems.

-   MEP --- Mechanical, Electrical & Plumbing. The category covering
    generators, HVAC, electrical and plumbing work.

-   BMS --- Building Management System. The central system that monitors
    a building\'s mechanical/electrical equipment.

-   LV Room --- Low Voltage Room. A dedicated room housing a building\'s
    electrical/network equipment.

-   SIRA --- Security Industries Regulatory Agency, Dubai\'s regulator
    for security systems and companies.

-   DET --- Dubai Department of Economy and Tourism, which issues trade
    licenses.

-   TRN --- Tax Registration Number, a company\'s UAE VAT registration
    number.
